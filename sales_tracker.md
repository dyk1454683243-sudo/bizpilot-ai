## 🚦 Pipeline Status Reference

* **Lead**: Identified business matching target segments (coaching, salon, clinic, repair, retail).
* **Contacted**: Outreach message sent (WhatsApp, email, or cold call completed).
* **Demo Scheduled**: Client agreed to a live walkthrough (in-person or Zoom/Loom).
* **Proposal Sent**: Pricing details and customization list submitted.
* **Negotiating**: Reviewing pricing packages or retainer support levels.
* **Won / Active**: Initial setup fee collected, custom app deployed, and configuration complete.
* **Lost / Inactive**: No response or declined offer.
